# Seven ways to practise design

## Agreed scope
Build seven fictional specialist projects inside the existing Next.js portfolio. Keep the portfolio's shared shell and file-based content. Each project has its own scoped tokens, art direction, substantive case study, product artifact and small working interaction. No claims of real clients, research participants, shipped results, live AI integrations or measured business impact.

## Sequence
1. Relay — AI product design. Incident-response agent: prompt contracts, context provenance, state graph, permissioned MCP tools, execution harness and deterministic evaluation fixtures.
2. Tandem — senior product design. Shift cover without hidden staffing gaps: visible constraints, progressive disclosure and reversible decisions.
3. Formwork — product design engineering. A component workbench connecting design decisions to accessible production code.
4. Common Ground — design research. Household energy upgrades: recruit for differences, distinguish observation from interpretation and design a decision-changing study.
5. Plainly — content design. A support journey for people struggling with a bill: content modelling, comprehension and humane recovery.
6. Waypoint — design leadership. A shared platform strategy: decision rights, capacity, sequencing and explicit trade-offs.
7. Afterhours — agency. Independent cinema identity and booking: expressive brand, practical conversion and a constrained delivery plan.

## Implementation
- Optional frontmatter fields preserve support for personal case studies.
- All narrative, example labels and specimen data stay in content/.
- Project tokens live in app/tokens.css; layouts in app/project-worlds.css. Scoped themes inherit the visitor's light/dark preference.
- Native HTML/CSS artifacts remain editable; generated photography supplies the cinema and research stories.
- Local demos are explicitly simulations. Evaluation results are computed from declared fixture expectations, never presented as model performance.
- Home and Work display all seven in the requested order. Markdown, JSON-LD and AI exports preserve fiction labels.

## Verification
Run build and lint. Inspect all seven pages in browser, phone and desktop layouts, light and dark themes, keyboard controls and the two deeper demos. Document any environment limits. Keep local preview running; do not deploy or commit over the parallel starter work.
