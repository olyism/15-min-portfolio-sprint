# Showcase asset provenance

Both photographs were generated with the built-in image generation tool for these fictional teaching projects. They are not client photography or fieldwork evidence. Final assets are converted to WebP for the website. The original generated files remain in the Codex image output folder.

## Afterhours

- File: `public/images/afterhours-cinema.webp`
- Original: `exec-76e63737-0c60-4706-8f02-299c65815ac6.png`
- Prompt: Create a final website asset, landscape 3:2 photograph for a fictional independent cinema called Afterhours. Artistic cinematic photograph of an empty 1970s cinema auditorium seen from back, orderly deep burgundy velvet seats, an illuminated warm off-white blank screen, red curtains, one amber aisle light, subtle analog 35mm grain, atmospheric inky shadow, sophisticated film-festival art direction, no people, absolutely no text, no lettering, no logos. Compose screen in upper center and seats in lower half, wide architectural perspective.

## Common Ground

- File: `public/images/common-ground-house.webp`
- Original: `exec-368634de-097a-4235-b365-05d80c956c8a.png`
- Prompt: Website editorial photography asset for a fictional design research case study about home energy upgrades. Landscape 3:2. An ordinary Australian weatherboard home on an inner-suburban street, pale sage-green timber exterior, terracotta tiled roof, small front garden with native plants, a simple gate, dappled eucalyptus shadows in late afternoon sun. Observational documentary architecture photography, analogue film colour, muted olive and warm chalk palette, honest lived-in atmosphere, no people, no text or logos, not luxurious real estate, no diagram overlays. Straight-on eye-level composition with house centered and sufficient garden foreground.
