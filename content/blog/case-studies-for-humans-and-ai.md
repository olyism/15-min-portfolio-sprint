Hiring managers spend about a minute on a case study. More and more often, an AI tool reads it first and writes them a summary.

The good news is that both want the same thing: **a clear story with the outcome up front.**

## The cadence

1. **What:** the project in one line.
2. **Problem:** who was stuck, and what it cost.
3. **How I solved it:** the key decisions, and why.
4. **Impact:** numbers that tie back to money, time or risk.

Start each section with a one-line summary, then add the detail. Humans can skim, and machines can quote you accurately.

## Make it readable to machines

This site publishes an `llms.txt` file and structured data for every project, so AI tools get the facts straight from you instead of guessing.
