## How I write

- **Lead with the outcome.** "Onboarding dropped from 4 days to 40 minutes", not "I improved onboarding."
- **Use "I" for what I did, and "we" for what the team did.** Be honest about which is which.
- **One idea per sentence.** Cut adverbs. Cut "very".
- **Show the trade-off.** Say what I chose *not* to do, and why.

## Words I use

Shipped, tested, learned, cut, measured, partnered, prototype, outcome.

## Words I avoid

Synergy, leverage (as a verb), delight (unless it's measured), seamless, "passionate about", "user-centric".
